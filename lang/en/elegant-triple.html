Elegant Triple Room

Book This Room

Details

The 'Elegant Triple Room' at Hanyuan Phase II 'Yinshan Pavilion' is specially designed for small families, a trio of close friends, or traveling companions. Featuring a calm and understated wood-grain architectural concrete wall as its base, combined with warm solid wood furniture and modern minimalist lines, it creates a serene, elegant, and carefree relaxing holiday atmosphere.

The room is thoughtfully configured with one standard double bed and one comfortable single bed (one large bed and one small bed), eliminating the hassle and inconvenience of adding an extra bed, allowing every guest to enjoy spacious and independent resting space. The bedding features soft, breathable pure white cotton materials and high-support spring mattresses. The headboard area features a full wall of vertical solid wood slats, while rattan drawer nightstands and soft warm-light night lamps are placed between and on both sides of the beds. The ceiling is equipped with an exquisitely designed wooden ceiling fan and track lighting, accompanying you for a sweet and undisturbed sleep.

The room features large, bright, and transparent windows alongside floor-to-ceiling sliding doors. During the day, natural sunlight pours in, providing abundant lighting and excellent ventilation. Surrounded by lush greenery, simply draw the curtains to experience the carefree tranquility of Meinong's countryside.

The bathroom features an independent dry and wet separated shower design, paired with a sliding door to block moisture. The main wall and floor of the shower area are specially inlaid with blue and white retro classic geometric art tiles, contrasting beautifully with bright marble-patterned wall tiles and a black-framed round hanging mirror. It is equipped with a premium smart bidet toilet, a washbasin cabinet, and branded bath amenities, combining lifestyle artistic taste with high-standard cleanliness and comfort.

The room is additionally equipped with a multi-functional solid wood long desk/Vanity table, designer chair, driftwood-style suspended clothes hanger, arched full-length mirror, silent mini-fridge, electric kettle, and a wall-mounted smart LCD TV (supporting internet streaming media), providing a cozy and convenient modern accommodation experience. In addition, guests staying at the Yinshan Pavilion can freely use the exclusive Phase II shared island kitchen lounge area, enjoying the wonderful moments of slow-paced living.
