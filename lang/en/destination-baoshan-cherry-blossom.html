Erjituan Cherry Blossom Trail・Baoshan Cherry Blossom Park

Home Nearby Attractions Erjituan Cherry Blossom Trail

The most dreamy cherry blossom viewing spot in southern Taiwan・Baoshan Cherry Blossom Park, Taoyuan District

二集團andBaoshan Cherry Blossom Park位於Taoyuan District, Kaohsiung，是南台灣最具代表性的賞櫻勝地, at an elevation of about 800 至 1000 公尺，每年 1 至 2 月間山櫻花and河津櫻接力盛開，整片山谷被染成夢幻的粉紅色，形成一條又一條的櫻花隧道。這裡不僅是攝影愛好者追逐的私房秘境，更因為鄰近藤枝森林遊樂區、檨仔腳湖and六龜溫泉區，成為入住 Hanyuan B&B 旅客Winter 一日遊的絕佳選擇。

一、歷史沿革and布農族部落文化

Erjituan is a traditional Bunun tribe located in Taoyuan District, Kaohsiung City. In the early days, tribespeople migrated here from Wangxiang in Nantou and established a lifestyle based on slash-and-burn agriculture. Due to its high altitude and broad field of vision, it is also regarded by the tribespeople as a place protected by ancestral spirits. The place name "Erjituan" (Group Two) originated from the administrative division during the Japanese colonial period, when the Japanese divided the Bunun tribes in Taoyuan District into "Group One," "Group Two," and "Group Three," corresponding to different settlement systems. "Erjituan" was the centrally located tribe among them, hence its name.

The Baoshan Tribe (Bao-shan) is a Han Chinese settlement near Erjituan. Located on a river terrace of a tributary in the upper reaches of the Laonong River at an altitude of about 850 meters, the climate is cool and humid, making it very suitable for the growth of cherry trees. In the early days during the Japanese colonial period and the early post-war period, this area mainly planted temperate fruits such as peaches, pears, and apples, and was an important cash crop area in the mountainous areas of Kaohsiung. However, with the outflow of rural population, the fruit industry gradually declined. In the early 2010s, the Community Development Association began to plant a large number of cherry trees, creating today's famous "Baoshan Cherry Blossom Park" and successfully transforming into a sightseeing and leisure tribe. The "Taoyuan Cherry Blossom Season" event is held around the Lunar New Year every year, attracting tens of thousands of tourists up the mountain to enjoy the flowers.

二、櫻花品種and最佳觀賞期

The cherry blossom varieties in Erjituan and Baoshan Cherry Blossom Park are quite diverse, ranging from Taiwan cherry blossoms and Kawazu cherry blossoms to double cherry blossoms. By taking advantage of the difference in blooming periods of different varieties, a flower viewing period of up to about two months is created. Overall, the best viewing period is from late December to mid-to-late February of the following year:

  1. 12 to end of month 1 月初：山櫻花（緋寒櫻）：台灣本土原生櫻花品種，花色深桃紅、花期短暫但艷麗，是二集團最先登場的櫻花種類，常出現於低海拔道路兩側and部落入口處。
  2. 1 to mid-month 2 月中：河津櫻：來自日本靜岡縣河津町的早春櫻花，花色粉紅、花瓣較大且密集，是Baoshan Cherry Blossom Park最主要的櫻花品種，數量約 3000 棵以上，開花時形成夢幻的櫻花隧道。
  3. 2 月上至 3 月初：八重櫻：重瓣櫻花品種，花色桃紅、花瓣層次豐富，多分布於Baoshan Cherry Blossom Park較高處的觀景平台Surroundings，為賞櫻季的壓軸。

It is recommended to visit from late January to early February, so that you can enjoy the overlapping period of Kawazuzakura in full bloom and Yaezakura in bloom at the same time. You can also avoid the period when the mountain cherry blossoms have ended, and get the best light for taking photos in the cherry blossom tunnel.

三、交通routeand建議行程

From Hanyuan B&B 前往二集團andBaoshan Cherry Blossom Park，主要行駛route有兩條：

  1. route A：經六龜、藤枝（推薦route）: From the B&B via Highway 28 Line to Highway 27 甲線進入六龜區，Continue 台 27 線往桃源方向，於「寶來」or「桃源」指標轉進二集團部落，車程約 2 hours.沿途可順道遊覽 Shenwei Tiantai Temple、六龜溫泉區、十八羅漢山。
  2. route B：經藤枝森林遊樂區：於台 27 線轉進藤枝林道，經藤枝森林遊樂區後再下切至二集團，route較短但山路較陡，適合搭配藤枝步道健行的旅客。

Due to narrow and winding mountain roads, it is recommended to depart before 7:00 AM and prepare motion sickness medication, light raincoats, and warm clothing. During the peak season, there is a temporary parking lot at Baoshan Cherry Blossom Park, but parking spaces are limited, so it is recommended to arrive early before 9:00 AM. Recommended stay time from the B&B: including photography, walking, lunch, and return trip, the whole journey takes about 8 to 10 hours, suitable for a one-day trip.

四、必拍Attractionsand攝影秘境

Erjituan and Baoshan Cherry Blossom Park have several secret spots spread by word of mouth among photography enthusiasts:

  • Baoshan Cherry Blossom Park主步道：公園內約 1 公里長的櫻花隧道，兩側 3000 棵以上河津櫻盛開時形成粉紅色拱門，是最具代表性的拍攝地點，建議Morning 10 點前抵達以避開人潮。
  • 二集團部落櫻花巷：部落內小巷種植多棵山櫻花and河津櫻，可結合布農族傳統石板屋、織布工藝品作為前景拍攝，極具人文氛圍。
  • 制高點觀景台：公園最高處的觀景平台可俯瞰整個櫻花山谷and遠處中央山脈稜線，是拍攝大景的最佳Location，建議於黃昏前 1 小時抵達。
  • 溪流櫻花倒影：雨季過後溪水清澈，可拍攝櫻花在水面的倒影，是極為夢幻的構圖畫面。

攝影建議：使用 50mm 標準鏡or 70-200mm 中長焦段，並準備偏光鏡（CPL）以增加天空藍度and櫻花飽和度。最佳光線為Morning 9-11 點的側光，以及下午 3-5 點的逆光。

五、順遊建議：規劃南橫Winter 一日遊

If you're coming from Hanyuan B&B 安排兩天一夜的Winter 行程，建議可以將二集團賞櫻andNearby Attractions結合：

  • 第一站：Shenwei Tiantai Temple（Details）：南台灣最大一貫道道場，宏偉宮殿式建築值得順道參觀。

VI. Notes & Helpful Tips

  • 山區Winter 氣溫可能降至 5-10 度，建議攜帶保暖外套and毛帽。
  • 櫻花季期間車位一位難求，建議提早於 8-9 點抵達，or於前一天夜宿 Hanyuan B&B 隔天清晨上山。
  • 山區路面可能濕滑，請穿著防滑鞋，並避免進入非公告之步道。
  • 請勿攀折櫻花樹枝、攀爬樹幹拍照，共同維護部落環境。
  • Baoshan Cherry Blossom Park is a traditional domain of the Bunun tribe. Please respect local culture and daily life. You can ask for permission from the tribespeople before taking photos.
  • 山區手機收訊不穩定，建議事先下載離線地圖，並告知家人or朋友行程。

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are Erjituan and Baoshan Cherry Blossom Park the same place?

Erjituan is the name of a traditional Bunun tribe in Taoyuan District, while Baoshan Cherry Blossom Park is a cherry blossom sightseeing area located near Erjituan. The two are geographically adjacent and have similar altitudes (800-1000 meters). They are usually planned as the same cherry blossom viewing route and can be completed in a one-day trip.

二集團櫻花什麼時候開？最佳觀賞期是何時？

Mountain cherry blossoms (Horikanzakura) usually bloom first from the end of December to early January each year, while Kawazu cherry blossoms bloom in succession from mid-January to mid-February. The overall best viewing period is from early January to mid-February. It is recommended to go around the Lunar New Year to enjoy both cherry blossoms at one time.

How long does it take to drive from Meinong Hanyuan B&B to Erjituan?

From Meinong Hanyuan B&B, take Provincial Highway 28 and Provincial Highway 27A to connect to the Southern Cross-Island Highway (Provincial Highway 20) to enter Taoyuan District. The total length is about 75 kilometers, and the drive takes about 2 hours. It is recommended to depart at 6-7 AM to avoid traffic to Hehuan Mountain and grasp the best morning light for photographing cherry blossoms.

二集團賞櫻需要門票嗎？有哪些注意事項？

Erjituan and Baoshan Cherry Blossom Park are both open to the public for free, but please note: temperatures in the mountainous area are low, so it is recommended to bring a jacket; the roads may be slippery, so please wear non-slip shoes; parking spaces are limited during the peak cherry blossom season, so please arrive early. Please do not break cherry blossom branches or litter, and work together to maintain the tribal environment.
