Garden View Quad Room (Small)

Details

Enter Phase II「Yinshan Pavilion」Garden View Quad Room — refined wood-grain concrete walls, warm solid wood furniture and modern minimalist aesthetics create a serene, private retreat away from the bustle.

The room thoughtfully features two standard double beds, selecting comfortable and skin-friendly pure white cotton bedding and high-support spring mattresses. The bedside is equipped with warm reading lights and log tree-ring side tables, and the ceiling features a wooden styling ceiling fan and soft track lighting. Whether it's a small family trip or friends traveling together, you can wash away travel fatigue and get the deepest relaxing sleep here.

The most refreshing feature is the large floor-to-ceiling glass sliding doors and private landscape balcony facing the outdoor greenery. Opening the blackout curtains at the break of dawn, you can take in the panoramic view of Meinong's lush green rural fruit trees and the rolling mountains in the distance; leaning against the balcony in the afternoon, feeling the breeze, you can fully experience the slow-living beauty of southern Taiwan's nature in all seasons.

Upgraded bright, clean bathroom with wet/dry shower, premium smart bidet toilet, vanity, round hanging mirror and vintage patterned tile accent wall — combining artistic beauty with cleanliness.

The room is also equipped with a solid wood long desk/Dressing table, designer chair, driftwood-style hanging clothes rack, full-length mirror, silent mini-fridge, electric kettle, and wall-mounted smart LCD TV (supports online video streaming), allowing you to enjoy a convenient and high-quality modern accommodation experience while being close to nature. In addition, guests staying at the Yinshan Pavilion can also freely use the exclusive phase-two shared island lounge kitchen space to enjoy a slow and relaxing wonderful time.
