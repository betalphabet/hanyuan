Garden View Quad Room (Large)

Book This Room

Details

The 'Garden View Quad Room (Large)' in Hanyuan Phase II 'Yinshan Pavilion' offers the most spacious living layout in the entire pavilion. With a serene and modern wood-grain fair-faced concrete textured wall as the base tone, it blends warm solid wood furniture with a retro arched rattan screen, elegantly separating the functional and multi-layered sleeping and living room lounge areas.

The sleeping area is configured with two standard double beds, featuring skin-friendly and breathable pure white cotton bedding and high-support spine-care spring mattresses. The bedside features selected wooden grille panels paired with warm and soft spherical wall lamps and log tree-ring bedside tables. The ceiling is equipped with an exquisite wooden ceiling fan and track lighting, creating a tranquil and relaxing premium atmosphere, allowing families and friends to enjoy a deep sleep all night.

A dedicated living room lounge sofa area is specially designed in the room, furnished with a high-quality peacock blue double fabric sofa, art paintings, a coffee table, and a solid wood bench. Leaning against the large transparent floor-to-ceiling glass sliding doors and private landscape balcony, you can welcome the fresh morning light and breeze of Meinong, overlook the lush rural fruit trees and magnificent rolling green mountains, and enjoy a relaxing tea time.

The bathroom space has been comprehensively upgraded, featuring a dry and wet separated independent shower enclosure design. The floor is paved with Spanish-style retro geometric patterned tiles, paired with elegant marble-textured wall tiles and large black aluminum-framed external ventilation windows, combining abundant natural light and air circulation. It is meticulously equipped with an advanced smart bidet toilet, a washbasin cabinet, and a round hanging vanity mirror, providing top-tier bathroom enjoyment like a star-rated hotel.

The room is also equipped with a solid wood long desk/Dressing table, designer armchair, driftwood-style hanging clothes rack, full-length mirror, silent mini-fridge, electric kettle, and wall-mounted smart LCD TV (supports online video streaming), balancing vacation leisure with modern convenient living needs. In addition, guests staying at Yinshan Pavilion can also freely use the exclusive shared kitchen space with an island in Phase II, sharing beautiful moments of slow and relaxed living.
