Location

Address: No. 7-11, Wugu Street, Zhongtan Village, Meinong District, Kaohsiung
Phone: 0933610333 / 07-6832493
Coordinates: 22°52'03.8"N | 120°32'50.5"E


Surroundings :

Close to city but away from bustle — within 2 km of 7-11, pharmacies and many Hakka restaurants, giving you countryside charm with everyday convenience.


Public Transit :

From Kaohsiung, take Kaohsiung Bus to Meinong Station, then transfer to a taxi to reach the B&B.
Alternatively, take the Qimei Express bus from HSR Zuoying Station to Meinong Station, then a taxi.


iRent Rental :

You can useiRent app to find a convenient pickup; the closest station is Qishan South.


Daily Car Rental :

From HSR Zuoying Station, you can book via IWS Rental 、 AVIS、Go Rental、Chailease Rental to reserve a car in advance.


Self-Drive :

National Highway 1: Take the Dingxin System Interchange toward Highway 10 Qishan direction; about 40 min to Highway 10 Qishan end
National Highway 3: Take the Yanchao System Interchange toward Highway 10 Qishan direction; to Highway 10 Qishan end
After reaching Highway 10 Qishan end, follow the map above (GPS recommended to navigate to the B&B)


Parking :

Free parking for cars and scooters.


EV Charging Station :

J1772 - 32A - 7kW EV Charger available(Noodoe EV)。


Private Driver:

SUV transfer service available for large luggage; within Kaohsiung area (additional fee)
Personal driver tour-guide service also available; please call for inquiries (additional fee)
